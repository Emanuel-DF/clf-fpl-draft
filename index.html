📜 Official League Rules

Weekly Transfers

  • 1 transfer per week from the remaining unpicked player pool.

Forced Transfers

  • Occurs on GW 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35.
  • The bottom two managers steal a player from the top two.

Injuries, Suspensions, AFCON & Club World Cup (Starts GW5)

  • Up to 4 red-flagged (injured/suspended/absent) players = 1 extra transfer that GW (executed after normal transfers).
  • Players away for AFCON / Club World Cup must be transferred out first.
  • Injured/suspended players count toward the 4 red flags.
  • All other players get an optional additional transfer.

Players Leaving the Premier League

  • Free transfer granted to remove them, processed after normal GW transfers.

Player Trades

  • Up to 2 players per team per GW proposal.
  • Non-trading teams receive 2 draft picks (instead of 1) if a 2-player trade is executed.

Ties during Forced Transfer GWs

  • Top (1st–3rd+ tied): Lowest scorer in that GW gets a player stolen. If still tied, use previous week's lowest score.
  • Mid (7th–9th+ tied): Highest scorer in that GW gets to steal from top two. If still tied, use previous week's highest score.
🔴 Red Autopicked Players from Draft

Players who miss the preseason draft receive extra transfers (processed immediately after everyone’s regular GW1 transfers) to swap out red auto-picked players to prevent exploiting new PL signings.

Order: Lowest to highest in the league table (ties broken by a random wheel). If a manager has multiple extra transfers, picks alternate sequentially rather than all at once.
